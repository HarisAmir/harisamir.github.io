# Affiliation logo sources

- Volkswagen: https://www.volkswagen.de/etc.clientlibs/clientlibs/vwa-ngw18/ngw18-frontend/apps/resources/statics/favicons/vw_bw.svg — simple emblem without text.
- RPTU: https://rptu.de/_assets/ae79e8fe97b597a30b2599e4220784f5/Images/RPTU_logo.svg — an intrinsic viewBox was added for responsive rendering.
- DFKI: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dfki-ric/better_launch/main/docs/assets/images/dfki.png — transparent, high-resolution asset from DFKI Robotics Innovation Center. CSS displays the standalone emblem on the left without the small bilingual descriptor.

The logos identify Haris's affiliations. They are displayed in monochrome with descriptive alt text. Original rights remain with their respective organizations.
